How projects work

  • Client and creator agree on the price, deposit, number of revisions, duration and first-cut date in the project chat. Those agreed terms form the contract for that project.
  • The client pays the deposit into escrow. Approving the first cut releases the deposit to the creator; rejecting it refunds the client.
  • Revisions are limited to the agreed number. On final approval the client pays the balance, and the clean master is released.
  • Rolvo keeps a 1% commission on each amount released to the creator.
  • The client pays the card processing fee charged by the payment processor on top of the order amount. It is shown before every payment.
  • Creators withdraw their balance to their own bank account or debit card. Standard withdrawals are free; instant withdrawals cost 1% of the amount.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe (card) or PayPal. Their own terms also apply. Rolvo holds escrowed funds until they're released or refunded under the agreed terms.

Content and rights

  • Creators keep ownership of their work until the final payment. On final payment, the client receives the rights agreed in the project terms.
  • Previews are watermarked and may not be downloaded, recorded or used before final payment.
  • Creators confirm they have the rights to everything they deliver, including any material used to generate it.

Leaving a contract early

Either side may end a project early. Funds are released or refunded according to the stage reached. To protect both sides, accounts are limited in how many projects they can end early within 30 days.
