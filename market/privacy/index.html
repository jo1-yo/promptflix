Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Rolvo connects clients with AI filmmakers and holds project payments in escrow. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. Your email address or phone number. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile and project details. What you tell us during onboarding or in an application: company, website, role, budget, timing, styles, rates and time zone.
  • Messages and files. Chats, proposed terms and the cuts creators upload to a project.
  • Payments. Amount, method (card or PayPal) and the processor's reference. Card numbers and PayPal logins are entered with Stripe or PayPal directly; they never reach Rolvo.

How we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To verify clients (for example, matching a work email to the company's website) and to review creator applications.
  • To run projects: messages, deliveries, escrow, refunds and payouts.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to resolve disputes.

We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Google user data

When you choose Sign in with Google, we request only your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) to create and identify your account. We don't request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google service. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who we share it with

  • The other side of your project. Clients and creators see each other's name, profile and project messages.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: Supabase (accounts and data), Stripe and PayPal (payments), and SMS and email providers that deliver sign-in codes.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Creators listed from public work

Some creator profiles are built from films the creator published publicly on YouTube or X, with a link back to the original. These profiles say they haven't joined Rolvo. A creator can claim their profile, or ask us to remove it, at the address below.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is open. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. You can ask us for a copy of your data or to delete your account at any time.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember your progress. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
